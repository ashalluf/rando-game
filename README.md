# shots/pedestrian-flow (fleet wave 4)

The crowd's flow (CrowdFlow). opengl3 stills (Compatibility renderer), downtown at Flower near 9th St, 11:00.
Staged with `FLOW=kerb|groups` on `tools/glshot/still_shot.gd`; the before is the same staging with `CROWD_FLOW=0`.

- `01_before_kerb.jpg` - nine people waiting at a red: they stand inside each other in one clump.
- `02_after_kerb.jpg` - the same nine: kerb slots along the crosswalk and a row back, spread away from the corner.
- `03_before_groups.jpg` - people walking toward the camera on the pavement: everyone alone, two walking through each other.
- `04_after_groups.jpg` - the same staging with the flow: a three walking abreast with its leader, the others stepping round them.
