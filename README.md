# trees-closeup stills

Before / after pairs of `tools/trees_closeup/tree_shot.gd` (a pavement with three scanned street
trees, the jacaranda, two palms and three code-built LA trees), Forward+ under lavapipe with TAA,
1280x720. "before" is `TREES_CLOSEUP=0`. Wind differs frame to frame (shader TIME), so crowns
are not in the same place in a pair.

- 01/02 street at noon, 03/04 a crown from the pavement, 05/06 the jacaranda,
  07/08 golden hour with the sun behind the row, 09/10 a palm at golden hour from below,
  11/12 night under four sodium lamps.
