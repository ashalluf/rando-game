# shots/rc5-oil-islands-dusk

The new reference camera `oil_islands_dusk` (tools/refcams/cameras_w8.json): the oil islands at
18:45 clear from the Long Beach sand. opengl3 refcam stills (1280x720) unless noted.

- `00_before_long_beach_from_above_1300.jpg` - main, 13:00, from 25 m over the Long Beach shore: no
  sand at all, only road strips (one on the waterline) and the horizon plane's glossy "water" sheets.
- `00_before_long_beach_sand_1300.jpg` - main, 13:00, eye level on the same shore.
- `01_before_oil_islands_dusk.jpg` - main (LONG_BEACH_SAND=0 RC5_OIL_ISLANDS=0), the final camera.
- `02_after_oil_islands_dusk.jpg` - this branch: the sand, the islands' floods in the bay, the
  towers lit up from their foot with dark crowns, the cranes on the right.
- `03_before_islands_crop.jpg` / `04_after_islands_crop.jpg` - the islands and the bay enlarged.
- `05_forward_plus_island_towers_before_top_after_bottom.jpg` - Forward+ (lavapipe), KESTREL alone
  at night (tools/glshot/far_landmark_shot.gd): top the old floods, bottom the dusk look.
