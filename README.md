# shots/rc5-buskers-plaza

New wave-8 reference camera `buskers_plaza` (tools/refcams/cameras_w8.json): street performers on
the downtown plaza (Pershing Square's block) at 15:30 clear, eye level in the crowd. opengl3
(Compatibility) stills, 1280x720, through `tools/refcams/refcams.py`.

- `01_before_main_buskers_plaza_1530.jpg` - main as it is (RC5_BUSKERS=0, nothing staged): one
  drummer on an empty plaza, the fountain a flat cyan disc.
- `02_before_staged_acts_RC5_BUSKERS0_1530.jpg` - the same acts staged (breakdancer in a freeze,
  mariachi trio, living statue) with this branch's fixes off: the dancer on bare setts (the
  cardboard buried under the plaza's paving), no passers-by on the square, the cyan disc.
- `03_after_buskers_plaza_1530.jpg` - this branch: the cardboard on the paving, a fuller ring,
  the plaza's own passers-by, the fountain a stone basin with jets and falling streams, three
  different mariachis.
- `04_detail_before_over_after_ring_and_fountain.jpg` - the middle of 02 over the same of 03.
