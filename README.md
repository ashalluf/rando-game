# drive-collide stills (tools/drive_collide/shot.tscn, opengl3, a bare deck)

Each is a 2x2 frame strip (left to right, top to bottom) of one probe scenario.

- 01 / 02: the player's red sedan coasting at 50 km/h into a stopped blue TRAFFIC car 1.2 m off
  centre. Before: the red car stops dead against it and the blue car never moves (kinematic =
  infinite mass). After: the blue car is shoved out of the traffic and slews; the red car keeps
  rolling (4.7 m/s, the blue 7.6 m/s, moved 8.5 m).
- 03 / 04: 50 km/h into a long wall at 30 degrees. Before: stopped dead, nose 30 deg in the wall
  (0.0 m/s). After: the car scrapes along the wall with its nose turned onto it and drives on
  (7.2 m/s after 2 s of coasting, which alone leaves 9.9).
