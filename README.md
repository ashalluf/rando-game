# rc-beach stills (wave 4)

Reference camera `beach` (the waterline at z 800, 14:00 clear) and views round it from the same spot,
opengl3 (Compatibility renderer, not the Mac's Forward+). Before = main / `SAND_ROADS=0 WADERS=0`.

- 01 / 02: the refcam before / after. Its frame is mostly sea, foam and sky (water-review's and
  sky-day-review's); after, a swimmer and a pelican out to the right. The near water stays empty in
  this narrow window: the frame only covers ~40 m of shore.
- 03 / 04: looking inland from the waterline: a traffic car parked on the sand at the water's edge
  (a grid street runs on under the beach), gone after (SandRoads).
- 05 / 06: 30 m up: a bus on the sand by the volleyball courts and palms in the middle of the sand;
  after, no bus, the palms along the back by the path.
- 07 / 08: along the shore south: empty water before; people wading knee-deep after (Waders).
- 09 / 10: close to a swimmer: before only the crown of the head clears the surf zone's water;
  after, head and shoulders out, floated on the drawn sea (SeaSurface), more swimmers.
- 11 / 12: along the shore north: a palm stood among the towels a dozen metres from the water;
  after, the palms along the back by the path.
- 13-15: the final after views on the tree merged with main (batch 26).
- 16: all ten refcams after; 17: the compare against `SAND_ROADS=0 WADERS=0` (= main): nine
  unchanged, freeway flagged only because a semi passed the camera in one run (traffic noise).
