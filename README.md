# car-crash-3 stills (wave 4)

Crashes at speed (CrashBreakup). opengl3 (Compatibility) stills from tools/glshot/car_shot.gd on a
bare ground plane, a sedan staged through the game's own crash path (CarDamage.stage, the same
take_hit a real crash takes). "before" = `CRASH_BREAKUP=0` (main's crumple only).

- 01 / 02: head-on into a wall at 70 km/h. After: the bonnet's catch lets go and it springs open
  (its primer underside drawn, an engine bay under it), the side glass bursts, bumper / grille /
  trim pieces lie on the road and stay.
- 03 / 04: T-bone at 80 km/h on the right doors. After: the glass facing it bursts out and the car
  is tripped onto its side by the physics (raised centre of mass + the trip's roll).
- 05: offset hit on the front right at 75 km/h: the front wheel torn off (it rolls away as a body,
  just out of frame left), the corner down on its hub.
- 06: the driver's airbag (white, in the cabin trace) through the burst side window.
- 07: night: the bare hub dragging on the road throws sparks (staged as if moving at 50 km/h).
- 08: day: a hanging bumper corner and the hub dragging sparks (staged as if at 50 km/h).
- 09: the T-bone at 80 km/h, 0.2 s / 0.5 s / 1 s after the hit, from the front: the car tripped
  over onto its side by the physics.
