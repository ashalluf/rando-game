# shots/rc5-rail-aerial

Wave 8 refcam `rail_aerial` (tools/refcams/cameras_w8.json): the Coral Line's aerial structure where
it swings off Flower over the 110, from River Blvd below, 20:00 clear, a train staged on the curve
(`rail_s` "2470:1"). opengl3 stills (Compatibility renderer), 1280x720.

- `01_before_rail_aerial.jpg` - main (6f5af3d2): the girder an unlit grey band with a rust blotch
  every 12 m, no light on the structure, the overhead wires a few broken dots, no poles to be seen.
- `02_after_rail_aerial.jpg` - the branch (before merging main): walkway lamps on the poles, the
  downlights under the girder and their pool on the street, the column top washed, the overhead
  wires lit near each pole (ribbons, not dotted prisms).
- `03_before_crop.jpg` / `04_after_crop.jpg` - the structure and the train at native pixels.
- `05_forward_vs_compat_pieces.jpg` - tools/rc5_rail_aerial/fwd_scene.tscn: the column, soffit, pole,
  lens and wires on the real materials, Compatibility (left) vs Forward+ under lavapipe (right).
- `06_after_structure_from_above_framing.jpg` - a framing still from 27 m over River Blvd (an early
  pass with stronger washes): the ballast pools, poles, lamps and the downlight pools along Flower.
- `07_final_after_merge_rail_aerial.jpg` - the final head (main merged, RcRailCrossing's wires
  elsewhere, RcRailAerial's lit ribbons on the structure): the catenary over the curve now reads.
