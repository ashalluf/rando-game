# shots/hero-moves (branch wt/hero-moves)

opengl3 (Compatibility) stills from `tools/glshot/hero_moves_shot.gd`, the hero_shot room with
the city's AgX grade; not the Mac's Forward+. "front" = camera in front of him, "side" = his right.

- contact_sheet.jpg - most of the below in one image
- idle_look_* / idle_stretch_* / idle_watch_* / idle_neck_* - the four idle variants mid-move
- takeoff_* - the jump's take-off (jump_start, 0.04 s in)
- fall_* - the fall pose at 40 m/s: left arm out, gun out to his right
- hero_landing_* - an 80 m/s landing standing still (land_hero, gun in against the thigh)
- landing_roll_* - an 80 m/s landing at a run (the roll)
- flight_* - boost-flying level at 40 m/s: laid along the velocity, the gun fist forward
- flight_banked_turn_a/b - flying a 70 deg/s turn (banked), from ahead and behind
- hit_front_* / hit_left_* - 0.08 s after a round from his front / left (the flinch)
- draw_* - 0.12 s into a weapon change (the launcher coming up from the hip)
- aim_rocket_* - the launcher raised after the draw
- sprint_side - the sprint on the ground at 13 m/s
- gun_stance_aim_front/side, gun_stance_idle_after - the AK's aim and hip carry with all of the
  moves in place: the stance, the IK and the hands still hold the gun
