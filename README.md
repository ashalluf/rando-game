# rc5-lighthouse-night stills

The new reference camera `lighthouse_night` (tools/refcams/cameras_w8.json): Gull Point light at 22:00
clear, seen from the breakwater's armour stones looking north-west over the harbour, the lens held at
`lighthouse_t` 19.8 s so the beam swings out over the water toward the lens. opengl3 (Compatibility)
refcams stills, 1280x720.

- `01_before_lighthouse_night.jpg` - main: the beam a solid tube of one brightness with a hard rim,
  the lantern not a light at all, paper-white lime on the stones, white foam sheets at the foot.
- `02_after_lighthouse_night.jpg` - the beam is light scattered in a soft pencil (bright near the lens
  and toward the eye, patchy haze, fading), the lantern glows, rock-faced stones, lace foam.
- `03/04_*_lantern_crop.jpg` - the lighthouse x4: the lantern's lamp now shows (it was hidden behind
  the lens barrel).
- `05/06_*_armour_foam_crop.jpg` - the foreground stones and foam x2.
- `07/08_fwdplus_*_foam.jpg` - Forward+ (lavapipe, fwd_block_shot.tscn: the chunks round the eye
  only, so no headland or lighthouse), the camera's eye, RC5_LIGHTHOUSE=0 / 1: the surge foam as
  white sheets, then lace.
- `09/10_fwdplus_*_cap_stones.jpg` - Forward+, on the breakwater's cap: the lime patches on the
  stones, then speckle.
