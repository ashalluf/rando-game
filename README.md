# rc5-construction-dusk stills

Refcam5 `construction_dusk` (tools/refcams/cameras_w8.json): a downtown tower going up, 18:45 clear,
from the pavement across the street from its hoarding and gate. opengl3 (Compatibility) stills.
Before = `CONSTRUCTION_DUSK=0` (main's look), after = this branch.

- 01_before_construction_dusk.jpg / 02_after_construction_dusk.jpg - the camera.
- 03_before_crop_gate.jpg / 04_after_crop_gate.jpg - the hoarding, gate and walkway, cropped:
  the blank poster blocks gone, printed bills, the late crew in hi-vis at the gate.
- 05_after_printed_posters_kit.jpg - the hoarding panel alone (tools/construction/kit_shot.gd), by day.

- `06_final_construction_dusk.jpg`: the final view after the merge-ready branch (glass at roughness 0.06, a quarter of the renderer's specular). The round glare at the clad top is in the before shot too (a light's clipped highlight on the glass): not fixed.
- `07_ten_cameras_off_vs_on.jpg`: the ten cameras of cameras.json with CONSTRUCTION_DUSK=0 (top) and on (bottom). street_noon / street_night flag on live traffic (the bus stands elsewhere); no construction site is in any of the ten frames.
