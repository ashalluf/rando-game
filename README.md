# shots/rc5-crash-closeup

Refcam `crash_closeup` (tools/refcams/cameras_w8.json, `"stage": "crash"`): a sedan that jumped the kerb on Cedar Ave (midtown) and wrapped its nose round a street lamp, 13:00 clear, from the pavement ahead of the nose. opengl3 (Compatibility) stills unless named Forward+.

- `01_before_crash_closeup.jpg` - first framing on main's crash code: the bonnet swung wide open over a black engine bay, a flat full-width crush with torn teeth down the wing, the bumper hanging like cloth, the lower grille standing whole in front of the crushed nose, nothing on the ground.
- `02_before_kit_pole55_carshot.jpg` / `03_after_kit_pole55_carshot.jpg` - the same hit on the car alone (car_shot.gd `DAMAGE=pole55`), before and after.
- `04_before_crash_closeup_ab_off.jpg` - the final framing with `CRASH_AFTERMATH=0` (the A/B: main's crash code): open bonnet, grille slab through the pole.
- `05_after_crash_closeup.jpg` - after: the nose bent into a V round the pole, the bonnet popped and buckled, the grille and bumper wrapped, oil and green coolant on the paving, radiator steam rising off the windscreen. (A walker passing through the car in this frame is hidden by the stager in the committed version.)
- `06_forwardplus_kit_before_after.jpg` - Forward+ (lavapipe), the car alone, CRASH_AFTERMATH 0 | 1.
- `07_after_ten_cameras_sheet.jpg` - the ten original cameras plus crash_closeup with the change on; compare against the same run with CRASH_AFTERMATH=0: the ten are unchanged (street_noon and freeway flagged only by live traffic differing run to run).
