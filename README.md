# shots/police-chase-2

The police chase (wt/police-chase-2). opengl3 stills (the Compatibility renderer), 1280x720, Flower St
at Olympic, staged with `CHASE=<scene>` on tools/glshot/still_shot.gd.

- `01_before_doors_noon.jpg` - POLICE_CHASE=0: a cruiser stopped facing the player, doors shut, the
  crew at the car's front end.
- `02_after_doors_noon.jpg` - the same: the driver's door swung open and held, the officer behind it
  aiming down the street at the player (the far-side officer behind the passenger door).
- `03_after_box_noon.jpg` - a stopped car (red) boxed in by three cruisers, front, rear and side, an
  officer behind the side cruiser's open door.
- `05_after_pit_noon.jpg` - the PIT: the cruiser turned into the blue car's rear quarter at 17 m/s; the
  car spins out flat, tyre smoke and skid marks, the cruiser easing off behind it.
- `06_after_spikes_noon.jpg` - a roadblock's spike strip (foreground: the scissor lattice, yellow pivot
  blocks, steel spikes) and the white car that just crossed it sliding on four flat tyres toward the
  roadblock, flares and officers.
- `07_after_doors_night.jpg` - the door cover at 21:30.
- `08_after_spikes_into_roadblock.jpg` - a second later in another take: on four flats the car could not
  stop and ploughed into a roadblock cruiser.

All staged with tools/police_chase/stage.gd at --spawn=2359.4,880,0,0 (opengl3: not what the Mac draws
for lighting). There is no still of the radio (sound) or the helicopter light's lead.
