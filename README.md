# shots/rc5-storm-downtown

New reference camera `storm_downtown` (tools/refcams/cameras_w8.json): Flower St just south of 8th,
looking north to the towers, 20:30 in a thunderstorm, a strike held for the still, staged traffic.
opengl3 refcam stills (the Compatibility renderer), 1280x720.

- 01_before_storm_downtown_flash.jpg - the camera on main (RC5_STORM=0): the held night strike lights
  the street like an overcast noon (p50 167), white rods down the road under every lamp, black
  litter discs in the sky.
- 02_after_storm_downtown_flash.jpg - after: the flash is a cold strike over a night street (p50 88),
  the towers silhouetted against the lit cloud round the bolt, the lamp reflections broken by the
  rain, the litter low.
- 03_before_storm_downtown_between_strikes.jpg - the same view between strikes on main: the storm's
  night curtain is a warm orange veil with banded streaks over the towers.
- 05/06 - a small scene (fwd_block_shot, opengl3) between strikes, before / after: less of the
  curtain's veil, thin inner streaks, the lamps' streaks broken and dimmer.
