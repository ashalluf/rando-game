# Driving effects (branch wt/driving-fx) - stills

All opengl3 (Compatibility) stills from `tools/glshot/still_shot.gd` with `DRIVE=` (tools/glshot/drive_fx_stage.gd), 1280x720.
There is no "before" for these: before this branch a car left nothing behind.

- `burnout_low.jpg` - a burnout on Flower St at noon (throttle against the handbrake): smoke off the rear tyres. `DRIVE=burnout`, chase camera.
- `burnout_above.jpg` - the same burnout from above: rubber patches under the rear tyres (tread visible), and the slide the car made when it was boarded.
- `drift_low.jpg` - a drift arc down Flower St ending against a parked taxi (sparks off the contact), smoke and marks behind.
- `drift_above.jpg` - the drift from 30 m up: four arcs of rubber.
- `scrape_night.jpg` - 22:00, a car sliding on its roof: spark streaks and the spark light on the road.
- `sand_spray.jpg` - the beach (z 790), a car driving across the sand: the rooster tail of sand hanging behind it (the car at the right edge).
- `sand_tracks_earlier_shading.jpg` - an earlier run on the beach showing the tyre prints in the sand; the marks were blend_mul then (since replaced by a lit film, lighter).
