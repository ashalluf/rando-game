# news-crews stills

opengl3 (Compatibility renderer) stills, 1280x720, `tools/glshot/still_shot.gd` with
`NEWS=wide NEWS_ALL=1 NEWS_NIGHT=21.5 -- --spawn=-10,40,0,-5 --hour=15` (one city load), and the
van alone with `NEWS=0 tools/glshot/car_shot.gd --row=0,0`.

- `00_before_first_cut_wide_1500.jpg` - the paused session's first cut as it stood: crew and tape,
  the van out of frame, nothing at the scene.
- `01_wide_story_1500.jpg` - a story: a car burning in the street, PACIFICA 9's truck double-parked
  down the street with its microwave mast up (the striped pole), the reporter and camera behind the tape.
- `02_over_the_operators_shoulder_1500.jpg` - the live shot: the operator with the camera on his
  shoulder, the LED panel on its stand, the reporter with the station's mic, the tape behind her.
- `03_reporter_on_air_1500.jpg` - the reporter talking to camera, the tape and the street behind.
- `04_live_truck_mast_up_1500.jpg` - the live truck at the kerb, mast run out.
- `05_from_above_1500.jpg` - what the news helicopter sees.
- `06_wide_story_night_2130.jpg`, `07_crew_night_2130.jpg` - the same at 21:30 (stand light on).
- `08_vans_mast_and_dish_raised.jpg`, `09_vans_side.jpg` - SUNCOAST 4 (mast) and BASIN 7 (dish)
  with their uplinks raised.

- `10_live_shot_after_no_backpack_1500.jpg` - the live shot again after the dress fix: the operator's
  shoulder camera, the reporter on air, the wreck burning behind the tape.

Note: in 02 and 07 the operator wears the crowd's backpack roll; news crews no longer roll one
(reporters wear nothing on their heads, operators sometimes a cap) since these were taken.
