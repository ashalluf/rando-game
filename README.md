# rc4-river-night stills

The new reference camera `river_night` (tools/refcams/cameras_w7.json): the LA River bed beside the
low flow, 65 m south of the 6th St ribbon bridge, 21:30 clear. opengl3 (Compatibility) refcam runs.

- `01_before_river_night_2130.jpg` - main (RIVER_NIGHT=0): an unlit channel, LED strips that light nothing, black water.
- `02_after_river_night_2130.jpg` - this branch: the arches washed by their LEDs, fixtures under the deck pooling light on the bed, banks and piers, the low flow and the damp silt mirroring the lit bridge, the floor's ambient held down after dark.
- `03_before_forwardplus_lavapipe.jpg` / `04_after_forwardplus_lavapipe.jpg` - the same eye on Forward+ (what the Mac draws) under lavapipe, a small scene (the three chunks in frame, fwd_block_shot; no far city), RIVER_NIGHT=0 vs on.
