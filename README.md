# shots/rc5-marina-night

The new wave-8 reference camera `marina_night` (tools/refcams/cameras_w8.json): the marina at 21:00
clear from the north end of the east headwalk, looking west down the lane along the north quay.
opengl3 refcam stills (Compatibility renderer), 1280x720.

- `01_before_marina_night_2100.jpg` - MARINA_NIGHT=0 (main's look): the water mirrors no light and
  its chop's orange crazing reads as brick paving; the quay restaurants are a liquor store and a
  smoke shop; the seawall a clean pale slab; the furled jibs black-and-white barber poles.
- `02_after_marina_night_2100.jpg` - MarinaNight: every lamp, pedestal, anchor light and lit cabin
  laid in the water as a wavering column, the restaurants' lit fronts mirrored past the bulkhead,
  eateries (deli, bakery, coffee, sushi), a weathered seawall (tide zone, weed, joints, streaks),
  jib covers with overlapping turns, cabin windows as panes.
- `03_before_forward_plus_lavapipe.jpg` / `04_after_forward_plus_lavapipe.jpg` - the same view on
  Forward+ (the Mac's renderer) under lavapipe, the marina's blocks alone (fwd_block_shot.tscn,
  960x540): off, the lane is one flat brown sheet; on, the columns read at the same strength.
