# rc3-vernon-industrial stills

New reference camera `vernon_industrial` (tools/refcams/cameras_w6.json): a Vernon street at 14:00,
clear, eye level on a corner, truck courts behind chain-link, tilt-up warehouses. opengl3 stills
(the Compatibility renderer, not the Mac's Forward+).

- `01_before_vernon_industrial.jpg` - main (484a7897).
- `02_after_first_pass_vernon_industrial.jpg` - the first pass: trailers at most docks (conspicuity

  framed storefront glass, weathered panels. The dark car-shaped blob on the road is a separate bug
  being chased (a car's contact shadow with no car drawn).
- `03_crop_courts_before_top_after_bottom.jpg` - the courts and office, before (top) and after.
