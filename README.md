# shots/sea-life: life in the water (wt/sea-life)

All opengl3 (Compatibility) stills, 1280x720, hour 16 clear unless noted; the city's real chunks.
Staged with `SEA="kind@x,z,lead;..."` on tools/glshot/still_shot.gd (SeaLife holds its clock).

- 01_before_beach_empty_sea.jpg - SEA_LIFE=0, the beach camera of 02.
- 02_after_dolphins_leaping_beyond_the_surf.jpg - a bottlenose pod porpoising past the break line, splashes where they break the water.
- 03_after_pelican_line_gliding.jpg - a line of brown pelicans gliding low over the swell (the pier park behind).
- 04_after_pelican_plunging_line_over_surf.jpg - one pelican folded in its plunge, the rest of the line gliding over the surf (crop x2).
- 05_before_harbour_empty.jpg - SEA_LIFE=0, the harbour off the port's south quay.
- 06_after_sea_lions_on_harbour_buoy_day.jpg - the harbour buoy with its sea lions hauled out, heads up.
- 07_after_sea_lions_on_buoy_night.jpg - the same at 20:30.
- 08_after_grey_whale_blow_far_out.jpg - a grey whale's back and its V-shaped blow ~380 m out (crop x2).
- 09_after_pelicans_side_on.jpg - the line side-on, into the afternoon glare.
- 10/11/12_kit_* - the kit alone (tools/sea_life/kit_shot.tscn): sea lions on the buoy close up, dolphins and a splash, pelicans gliding / flapping / plunging / sitting.

Not seen on Forward+ (the Mac).
