# Rain crowd stills (wt/rain-crowd)

opengl3 (the Compatibility renderer, not the Mac's Forward+), downtown on Flower St near Olympic.
"after" shots are the same load staged with `RAIN_STAGE=<level>` (everyone near the player settled
as the rain would leave them); "before" is `RAIN_CROWD=0`, same weather, same EYE.

- `01_before_rain_street.jpg` / `02_after_rain_street.jpg` - rain at 15:00, along the east pavement.
- `03_after_storm_street.jpg` - a storm (level 1.0): the street empties out, the rest under umbrellas.
- `08_before_rain_along.jpg` / `09_after_rain_along.jpg` - a tighter view along the pavement: two
  women talking in the rain; after, the talk breaks up, one walks on under a yellow umbrella, the
  other has gone in; the old man holds a black umbrella over his head (final arm hold).
- `10_after_umbrella_close.jpg` - a compact umbrella held in the hand, close (RAIN_FOCUS=umbrella).
- `11_after_rain_night_along.jpg` - the same view at 21:00 in the rain.
- `12_after_storm_along.jpg` - the same view in a storm.
- `13_lab_umbrellas_held.jpg` - tools/crowd/crowd_lab.tscn SCENARIO=rain RAIN_GEAR=umbrella: the
  canopies over the heads, held up in the hand (walking, standing, jogging).
- `14_lab_hood_up.jpg` - the same lab, RAIN_GEAR=hood: a hood fitted to the rig's skull, pulled up.

01-03 were shot before the final arm hold (the canopy then sat a little lower); 08-14 are final.
