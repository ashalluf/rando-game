# rc5-driver-rain stills

The new wave-8 reference camera `driver_rain` (tools/refcams/cameras_w8.json): from just behind the
driver's eye in the player's own car (the real CarInteriors cabin), stopped second in the queue at a
red on Grand Ave downtown, 22:00, rain. opengl3 (Compatibility) stills from one city load; the
"before" is the same frame with `DRIVER_RAIN=0` (CABIN_AB=1 in tools/refcams/cabin_stage.gd).

- 01_before_driver_rain.jpg - main's look: a dry, clear windscreen in a downpour; the nav screen a
  pale daylight map; the cluster's middle a flat pale slab; brake-lit halos of the car ahead as
  salmon disks.
- 02_after_driver_rain.jpg - the rain on the glass from inside (beads, runs, the wipers' arcs, each
  bead refracting the street, the lights blooming in the water film), the cabin catching a little
  street light, night-mode screens, close lamp halos faded.
- 03_windscreen_before_top_after_bottom.jpg - the windscreen at full resolution, before over after.
- 04_dash_before_top_after_bottom.jpg - the dash: daylight screens vs night mode.
