# shots/rc5-club-line

The `club_line` reference camera (tools/refcams/cameras_w8.json): club AFTERGLOW's line on 6th St
between Grand and Olive at 23:30 clear, from the north pavement across the street. opengl3
(Compatibility) stills, 1280x720, from `refcams.py run --only club_line`.

- `00_main_first_framing.jpg` - main as it was, from my first (wider) eye: no beams, the club one
  more shop under the "STORE CLOSING" sign band.
- `01_before_club_line.jpg` - the final eye, `RC5_CLUB_LINE=0` (main's look; the staging - the
  opening and the two double-parked pickups - is on in both).
- `02_after_club_line.jpg` - the same eye with RcClubLine: the searchlight shafts from the lens,
  the club's fascia board with its name over the sign band, its light along the line.
- `03_club_front_before_after_crop.jpg` - the club front, before | after.
- `04_forward_plus_small_scene_before_after.jpg` - `tools/rc5_club_line/club_shot.tscn` on Forward+
  (lavapipe), before | after: the beams now leave the lens, the board keeps the club's amber.
