# shots/speedboats

Stills for `wt/speedboats` (drivable speedboat and jet ski). opengl3 (Compatibility) under Xvfb,
`tools/glshot/still_shot.gd BOAT=speedboat|jetski BOAT_VIEW=all --hour=17.4`; lineups from
`tools/boats/boat_shot.gd`. Not Forward+.

- 01_speedboat_chase_golden_1724.jpg - the SALTRUNNER 24 at ~16 m/s across the bay, the hero at the helm.
- 02_speedboat_side_sunset_1724.jpg - abeam toward the sun (the boat had moved on in this early run, so it sits at the frame's edge).
- 03_jetski_chase_golden_1724.jpg - the WAVEKITE turning, rider seated, wake foam and spray (the foam reads as blobs: the harness emits particles at ~1 fps).
- 04_jetski_side_coast_1724.jpg - the jet ski with the beach town and pier behind.
- 05_speedboat_wake_from_above.jpg - an early run: the wake's foam from above (boat out of frame, next to a pier).
- 06_boatkit_lineup_bow.jpg, 07_boatkit_speedboat_profile.jpg - the two meshes alone on flat water.
