# garage-drive-in stills (first batch)

Multi-storey car park downtown ("CIVIC PARK", block (22,-7), 6 decks), opengl3 / Compatibility
renderer (not the Mac's Forward+), 1280x720, 15:00. Sequence 02-06 is one real drive: the player in
a sedan, driven by tools/car_park/autopilot.gd with the player's own controls, from the street to the
roof (146 s of game time). Weather rolled to rain during the drive.

- 01_after_exterior_1500.jpg - the structure from the street: precast spandrels, columns with
  the level colour bands, the stair core with the P sign.
- 02_drive_barrier_ground.jpg - the entry island: ticket and pay machines, both arms lifted for the car.
- 03_drive_first_ramp.jpg - up the first ramp in the walled ramp strip.
- 04_drive_deck2.jpg - deck 2: the strip lane, ceiling fittings, parked cars.
- 05_drive_top_ramp.jpg - the last ramp up to the roof.
- 06_drive_roof.jpg - on the roof deck, parked cars, light poles.

(These were shot before the soffits got their painted-white emission.)
