# film-studio stills (opengl3 / Compatibility renderer, not the Mac's Forward+)

SUNSPIRE PICTURES, an invented Hollywood studio lot in midtown under the ridge sign
(3 x 2 blocks, x 580-796, z -767..-413 on the default seed).

- 01_before_aerial_noon: the same view with FILM_STUDIO=0 (a plaza and midtown blocks).
- 02_after_aerial_noon: the lot from the south-west: gate arch, office block, bungalows, water tower, numbered stages, basecamp trailers.
- 03_after_gate_street_noon: the main gate from the street: pylons, arch with gilt name and mark, guard booth, barrier arms.
- 04_after_backlot_new_york_street_1000: the backlot's New York street of false fronts (shopfronts, awnings, fire escapes, cast-iron lamps, parked cars).
- 05_after_water_tower_1300: the water tower with the studio's mark and name.
- 06_after_stage_elephant_doors_1700: stages 8 and 9, elephant doors, red lights, honeywagons and trailers.
- 07_after_stage4_gable_1100: a stage gable with its painted number and roof stair.
- 08_after_gate_night_2130: the gate at night, the gilt name and mark lit.
- 09_after_aerial_wide_noon: the lot in its neighbourhood (north half in LOD).
