# rc5-church-sunday stills

Refcam `church_sunday` (tools/refcams/cameras_w8.json): OUR LADY OF THE ARROYO, a Mission church in
the south basin, 10:30 on a Sunday, clear, from the far parking lane across the street. opengl3
(Compatibility) stills from refcams.py, 1280x720.

- `01_before_church_sunday.jpg` - main (`WORSHIP_SUNDAY=0 RC5_CHURCH=0`): the church hidden behind
  kerb and lawn trees, nobody coming to it on a Sunday morning.
- `02_after_church_sunday.jpg` - the branch: the front clear of trees, a congregation in Sunday
  clothes at the gate, on the path and the forecourt, the tower in stages with pilasters and a round
  light, no sky through the espadana's underside, darker stained glass by day, lime-washed stucco.
- `03_after_first_framing_from_left.jpg` - an earlier pass from the left: the tower's plain shaft
  merged with the facade into one white mass (what led to the tower dressing), and the sky line
  under the espadana.
