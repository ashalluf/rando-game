# shots/minimap (wt/minimap)

These are opengl3 stills (the Compatibility renderer under Xvfb + llvmpipe) at 1920x1080, made with
`tools/minimap/map_shot.gd`. The default seed is used, with the player on Grand Ave downtown at 13:00.

- 01_minimap_downtown_hud.jpg: the game with the HUD in CLEAN mode. The minimap is bottom right and shows a waypoint 600 m ahead.
- 02_minimap_downtown_crop.jpg: the minimap close up. It shows the GPS route (violet), a landmark glyph, the streets and Wilshire as an avenue.
- 03_map_basin.jpg: the full map zoomed out over the basin. It shows the hill shading and contours, the sea, freeway shields (the game's own numbers), the Coral Line and its stations, landmark glyphs, district names, the legend, the scale bar, and a waypoint 362 m north of the player.
- 04_map_downtown.jpg: the full map over downtown at 0.6 px/m, with street names.
- 05_map_downtown_zoom_labels.jpg: zoomed into downtown. It shows street names along the roads, Flower St's dashed underground Coral Line with the 7TH ST / FLOWER station, tower names, and the route.
- 06_map_basin_gps_route.jpg: a 3.7 km GPS route from downtown to Westlake across the basin. This is an earlier pass, before the street colours were fixed.
- 07_map_downtown_gps_route_before_street_fix.jpg: the same route zoomed into downtown. This is from before the change that draws downtown's 18-22 m streets as streets rather than avenues (the yellow mesh).
- 08_waypoint_beacon.jpg: the waypoint's beacon in the world, 600 m down the street, rising over the skyline.
- 09_waypoint_beacon_before_widen.jpg: the beacon before it was widened and coloured violet.
