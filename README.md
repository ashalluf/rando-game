# shots/ragdoll-feel (fleet wave 4)

Ragdolls that read right (RagdollFeel, branch `wt/ragdoll-feel`). Each image is 8 frames left to
right, top to bottom, from `tools/ragdoll_feel/feel_shot.tscn` (opengl3, a small street; physics is
renderer-independent). BEFORE = `RAGDOLL_FEEL=0` (the jointed dolls of section 9gk as they were).

- 01_before_rifle_round_in_chest.jpg - a rifle round throws the walker out of frame.
- 02_after_rifle_round_in_chest.jpg - recoil, a stagger back, sits down, falls on her back.
- 03_after_rifle_round_in_back.jpg - pitches forward, to her knees, face down.
- 04_after_head_shot.jpg - the head snaps, the knees go.
- 05_before_car_hit_14ms.jpg - a car at 14 m/s throws the body upright, ahead of it.
- 06_after_car_hit_14ms.jpg - legs swept, folds over the bonnet, rides it, slides off as the car brakes.
- 07_before_stairs.jpg - knocked at the top of ten steps: stops across the top steps.
- 08_after_stairs.jpg - tumbles down to the foot of the flight.
- 09_before_flung.jpg / 10_after_flung.jpg - a hard knock: before a stiff tumble, after trunk first with the limbs trailing.
