# shots/rc5-sea-of-clouds

New refcam5 `sea_of_clouds` (tools/refcams/cameras_w8.json): the brow below the east range's fire
lookout (~465 m), 08:30, weather marine, looking WNW over the basin. Before = SEA_OF_CLOUDS=0
(identical to main), after = the branch. opengl3 (Compatibility) stills.

- 01_before_sea_of_clouds.jpg - main: no deck at all from the hills (the eye stands between the
  deck's base 255 m and its flat 520 m top, which also buried every tower and the front range).
- 02_after_sea_of_clouds.jpg - the morning deck thins inland (430 m at the coast, 266 m inland):
  a white sea of stratus filling the basin, downtown's crowns poking through, puffed top, clear air.
- 03_after_deck_alone_deck_shot.jpg - tools/rc5_sea_of_clouds/deck_shot.tscn: the deck alone with
  test towers and a hill standing out of it (the soft shore round them).
