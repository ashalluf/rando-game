# rc3-freeway-night stills

The wave-6 refcam `freeway_night`: the 110 northbound at 22:00 clear, driver eye height, toward the downtown skyline (opengl3 1280x720).

- 00_before_unstaged_truck_on_lens.jpg - main, no staging: a box truck the load spawned sat on the lens.
- 01_before_freeway_night_2200.jpg - main with the staged traffic (freeway_cars key): flat blue deck, one warm strip over the inner lanes, unlit cars and barriers, tail lights gone past a few cars.
- 02_after_freeway_night_2200.jpg - this branch: each median standard throws a cobra-head pool (bright under it, darker between, outer lanes dimmer), real lights at the nearest heads light the cars, trucks, barriers and gantries, freeway cars' tail lights carry to 720 m.
- 03_first_pass_too_bright_2200.jpg - the first pass (pool gain 1.25, lights at full street-lamp gain): an overlit warm sheet, kept to show why the numbers were lowered.
- 04_before_traffic_ahead_crop.jpg / 05_after_traffic_ahead_crop.jpg - the traffic ahead, 3x: dark bodies before, lit cars and tail lights after.
- 06_before_over_after.jpg - before over after, half size.
- 07_ten_cameras_ab_compare.jpg - the ten original refcams, FREEWAY_NIGHT=0 over on, one tree: 7 identical; street_noon, street_night and freeway flagged by traffic moving (a bus in street_night), nothing of this change in them.

Not verified on Forward+ (the Mac's renderer): lavapipe ran out of the box's 15 GB even for a single block.
