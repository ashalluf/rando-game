# shots/coast-houses

The coast highway's beach houses rebuilt as Malibu beach houses (CoastHouses, wt/coast-houses).
opengl3 1280x720 (Compatibility renderer, not the Mac's Forward+). Before = `COAST_HOUSES=0`, same build.

- 01/02 `coast_highway` refcam (17:00 clear, from the seawall's apron): the houses are far off; near flat.
- 03/04 `coast_houses_sand` from the sand looking NE along the decks (17:00): setback upper floors over
  terraces, glass walls to the sea, cable rails on the timber houses, a glazed gable, shed and flat roofs.
- 05/06 `coast_houses_sea` from the swash, wide.
- 07/08 `coast_houses_dusk` the sand view at 19:45: the glass walls' traced rooms lit.
