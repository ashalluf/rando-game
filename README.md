# shots/rc5-airport-final

Refcam5 `airport_final` (tools/refcams/cameras_w8.json): an airliner on its turn onto final over
midtown at 19:30 clear, from the middle of Highland Blvd looking south up at it. opengl3 stills
from tools/refcams (1280x720). Before = `JET_FINAL=0`, same eye and staging.

- 00_first_framing_crest_ave.jpg - an earlier framing on Crest Ave (no overhead lines there; the
  eye moved to Highland Blvd, which has them on its east side).
- 01_before_airport_final.jpg / 02_after_airport_final.jpg - the camera, before / after.
- 03_before_jet_crop.jpg / 04_after_jet_crop.jpg - the jet, 6x: before, the wings and tailplane
  vanish into the sky, the nacelles' bellies and the gear are black blobs; after, the city's glow
  on everything facing down, the gear painted as gear (grey legs, black tyres round hubs) and lit
  by the landing lights.
