# car-variety-4: Cars, the fourth wave

Five new Blender-built everyday bodies (tools/make_cars_4.py): a compact wagon, a 2-door muscle coupe,
a luxury SUV, a kei-style city van and a 1970s sedan. All opengl3 (Compatibility) stills, not Forward+.

- `02_after_flower_st_queue_fourth_wave_bodies.jpg` - the same queue made of the five new bodies (`STREET_MIX=35,36,37,38,39`): the wagon nearest, then the luxury SUV, muscle coupe, classic and city van.
- `03`-`07` `_ingame_<body>_front_rear.jpg` - each body in the game (car_shot.gd), front and rear three-quarters, in a typical paint.
- `08`-`12` `_blender_preview_<body>.jpg` - the Blender (Cycles) previews from the generator. The muscle's and the classic's are from before the scoop was lowered and the bumpers slimmed; the in-game shots are after.
