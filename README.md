# shots/olvera: Paseo de las Golondrinas (the marketplace lane by Pueblo Station)

opengl3 (Compatibility) stills at 1280x720, not the Mac's Forward+. Same camera before / after
(`PUEBLO_LANE=0` is the before: the seeded block that stood there).

- 01 / 02: the plaza from its south-east corner, 15:30: the kiosk, the shade trees with ring
  benches, papel picado from the kiosk's eave to the poles, bulb strings.
- 03 / 04: down the lane from its south end, 15:30: the puestos under the vine pergola, papel
  picado across, the adobe's corredor on the right.
- 05 / 06: the block from the air: the church (front on the plaza), the plaza and kiosk, the lane
  under its pergola, the firehouse, the hotel block, the car parks.
- 07: the church facing the plaza (a sanctuary: no shooting at or into it).
- 08: the puestos close up: pinatas, maracas, pottery, sarapes, a vendor in her stall.
- 09: a sarape stall from the aisle, 11:00 (taken before the weave's moire fix).
- 10: the lane at 20:50: bulbs, warm pools on the bricks, the paper lit.
