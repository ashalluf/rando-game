# shots/ocean-traffic

Ocean traffic (wave 3, branch `wt/ocean-traffic`). City stills are opengl3 (Compatibility), 1280x720,
before = `OCEAN_TRAFFIC=0`, after = the same camera with `OCEAN_STAGE=-1250,-330,95` (seven boats staged
off the pier) plus the always-running fleet. Kit stills are `tools/ocean_traffic/vessel_shot.tscn`
(a patch of the real ocean shader, seconds).

- 01 / 02 pier end looking west, 15:00: sloops under sail (heeled, both tacks), a motor yacht.
- 03 / 04 the same at 20:42: masthead and nav lights.
- 05 / 06 aerial over the coast, 16:30: a sport fisher and its wake, a sloop.
- 07 / 08 the harbour mouth looking south over the bay, 17:36: the five container ships at anchor.
- 09 / 10 the same at 21:00: their anchor lights and deck floods.
- 11 the ferry, a tug and the pilot boat under way with their wakes (bow wave, prop wash, Kelvin arms).
- 12 four sloops on both tacks, heeled to leeward, the boom sheeted by the point of sail.
- 13 the PACIFIC TERN catamaran ferry.
- 14 the lineup from astern: wakes.
