# rc4-terminal-curb stills

New reference camera `terminal_curb` (tools/refcams/cameras_w7.json): the airport's departures
kerb at 08:00 clear, eye level under the terminal's wing roof looking east along the curb.
Shot with `REFCAMS_JSON=tools/refcams/cameras_w7.json python3 tools/refcams/refcams.py run <dir> --only terminal_curb`
(opengl3, the Compatibility renderer).

- `01_before_terminal_curb.jpg` - main: a bare grey slab, three people at the far end, the loop
  cars bumper to bumper, the houses across the lanes in plain view.
- `02_after_v1_terminal_curb.jpg` - first pass: concrete slabs, kerb stone, steel bollards, a door
  pylon, glass vestibules, travellers with luggage, palms on a median across the lanes, taxis.
- `03_after_terminal_curb.jpg` - final (eye moved 4.5 m west so no walker stands in the lens):
  taxis and white shuttle vans in the loop, kerb-lane cars standing on their hazards for drop-offs,
  sixty travellers in groups with their luggage along the kerb, door pylons, glass vestibules.
- `04_before_after_side_by_side.jpg` - main (left) against this branch (right). Note the eye moved
  between them (the before is from [-404, 627]).
